# Freestyle mode (optional, men's senior freestyle)

Status: design agreed 2026-11-13; engine + animations built 2026-11-13; season (RTC rosters, 4 events, Trials, Worlds/Olympics, honors, aging, prestige payoff, UI) built 2026-11-14. Not yet: watching freestyle bouts in the viewer, recruiting pitch topic, freestyle record books, injuries/dev bonus for college participants.

## Design (agreed with the user, don't re-litigate)
- **Optional**: a "Freestyle season" switch on the new-game screen, off by default. Off = the game is exactly as before (`newGame` identical for a seed). Freestyle pools (foreign wrestlers, real US seniors, RTC rosters) use their own rng stream so college generation is identical even with it on.
- **Flow**: folkstyle season -> folkstyle offseason (5 steps) -> freestyle season -> next folkstyle season.
- **Freestyle season** = RTC roster phase, then 4 events in order: Bill Farrell (real: November, the first US qualifier for the Trials; kept as the opener, nothing between NCAAs and the US Open fits better), US Open, World Team Trials (Final X folded in), World Championships. Olympic years (2028, 2032, ...) replace Worlds with the Olympics, still 10 weights, and the Trials are named the **Olympic Team Trials** (winners = "Olympic Team"; `evName(G, k, y)` / `teamName(G, y)` in freestyle.js, by the freestyle year shown, `G.fs.year`; profile results history names each year's Trials by its own year; 2026-11-14). Names only: same events, same rng.
- **RTC**: one per school, no size cap. The user manages his own (drop / pick up, entries, weight). AI RTCs take college wrestlers who are All-American level and up; current college wrestlers can only be at their school's RTC; recent graduates stay or move to another RTC. Not everyone has to enter every event.
- **Weights**: 1:1 map 125->57, 133->61, 141->65, 149->70, 157->74, 165->79, 174->86, 184->92, 197->97, 285->125 kg. One move (up or down a class) per freestyle season, chosen in the RTC roster phase, instant.
- **Trials qualification** (game mix of real + fictional): top 6 at Farrell or US Open, prior-year World/Olympic team members, prior-year NCAA champions, plus at-large bids. Trials = bracket; the final is best-of-3. A prior-year World/Olympic medalist at that weight gets a bye straight into the best-of-3 final against the Trials bracket winner. Only the Trials winner per weight goes to Worlds.
- **Real 2026 procedure for reference** (USA Wrestling): US Open champions -> Final X; Challenge Tournament qualifiers: prior World/Olympic team members, U23/U20 world medalists, highest-placed US athlete (top 4) at the Farrell, NCAA top 3, US Open top 7, Pan-Am medalists; 2025 world medalists get an automatic Final X berth (best of 3).
- **Payoff**: RTC results give a small prestige bump and a recruiting pitch topic.
- **Ages**: post-college wrestlers keep developing slowly, then decline from a certain age; big post-college jumps are rare. Retirement around 30-34.
- **Foreign wrestlers**: real countries, strength by real-life national strength, generated names.
- **Real US seniors**: scrape current US freestyle wrestlers not already in the game, rate them from results, assign them to the most plausible school RTC (approximate: real RTCs/clubs are not 1:1 with schools).
- **Generated RTC athletes**: schools with no (or few) real RTC athletes get a few generated post-college wrestlers, count and quality scaled by school prestige and the strength of its current roster (wrestler rankings).
- **Stats**: separate freestyle records, Hall of Fame, multi-time World champions / medalists.

## Rules implemented (UWW rulebook, Dec 2025 edition)
- 2 x 3:00, 0:30 break, all restarts standing, no choices, no overtime.
- Points: 1 step-out, reversal, lost challenge, fleeing a hold (+caution), passivity point; 2 takedown, exposure (each gut wrench / leg-lace turn); 4 feet-to-danger from standing, short-amplitude lift to danger, grand amplitude not to danger; 5 grand amplitude to direct danger.
- Par terre only after a takedown: top man works, ref stands them up when the attack stalls; escapes score nothing; no riding time.
- Passivity: verbal warning, then a 30 s activity period (passive man must score or the opponent gets 1); 0-0 at 2:00 of period 1 forces a call, and again with 1:30 left in period 2 (2026-11-14: before that ~1 bout in 10,000 ended 0-0); under 0:30 in a period, evading = caution + 1.
- Win: fall, 10-point technical superiority, 3 cautions, points. Tie criteria (checked against the rulebook 2026-11-14, "In case of a tie by points"): 1 highest-value action/hold, 2 number of highest-value actions/holds, 3 fewer cautions, 4 last technical point; only technical points count as holds (step-outs do; caution, passivity/activity and lost-challenge points don't); 4.1: no technical point at all (1-1 from two passivity points) -> the first point scored (`S.firstPt`); a 1-1 of passivity point vs step-out goes to the step-out (4.1.1, via rule 1). A coin flip remains only for 0-0 ('referee decision'), not seen in 20,000 bouts. Measured over 20,000 bouts (quality 13-18): 4.3% tied; decided by bigger move 40%, last technical point 34%, number of moves 21%, fewer cautions 4%, first point 2%.

## Engine (js/engine/fs.js, `simulateFsBout(wA, wB, opts)`)
- Same seeded rng and log format as bout.js (`type, period, clock, a, b, text, pos, fx, who, mv`, plus `cau` [cautions A, B] and `act` {who, left} for the activity clock; `ride` is always [0, 0]). Result adds `style: 'fs'`, `kg`, `cp` (UWW classification points winner:loser), `crit` (tie-break reason). Methods: Fall, Tech Fall (VSU, 10 points), Decision (incl. criteria), Disqualification (3 cautions). bout.js is untouched.
- Weight: `opts.kg`, else `FS_OF_LBS[w.weight]`.
- Composites (same 14 attributes): leg attack `.6 tdo .2 speed .1 hand .05 str .05 iq`; defence `.6 tdd .12 hand .1 speed .1 flex .08 iq`, through the folkstyle `pTakedown` curve (elite defence stays nearly unscorable). Par terre attack `.65 topt .2 str .1 iq .05 topc`, defence `.55 botRes .3 flex .15 str`. Push (step-outs) `.35 str .3 hand .2 cardio .15 tdo`. Throws `.5 big .3 str .2 speed`.
- Counters added 2026-11-14: `FS.fhl` .16 (x big) of counter takedowns are a front headlock (td 2, mv `fhl`) followed at once by a gator roll (55%) or head pinch exposure (2); `FS.wrap` .35 of counters to a high-crotch are a chest wrap / crotch lift roll-through (exposure 2 to the defender, no takedown, back to neutral). Big moves include a duck-under to a belly-to-back suplex (`bbsuplex`, 2/4/5). `FS.turn` lowered .20 -> .12 to keep the calibration with the chains.
- Flow: neutral ticks 7-12 s. An attack (`FS.act` x pace x plans) is a throw (`FS.big`, rises with big: 2 / 4 / 5 points, counters 2 or 4) or a leg attack (takedown 2; `FS.four` to the back for 4 off double / high-crotch / single / sweep; `FS.funk` both score 2; counters 2; drive-out step-out 1). Quiet ticks: step-out push (`FS.push`), hand fighting, passivity. Par terre after a takedown: 4-8 s ticks, turn chance `FS.turn` logistic on the par-terre edge (`turnK`), each further turn x `turnDecay`; after a turn he may keep the lock and roll again at once (`rollAgain`; `FS.again`: lace .5, gator .42, ankle lace .3, gut .26, pinch .22, arm trap .14; x 2 sig(.25 x edge) x `againK` .7 per extra roll; 2 s each; also after a gator roll / head pinch from the front headlock. Extra rolls per first roll, 4,000 bouts: lace .82, gator .58, ankle lace .41, gut .35, pinch .28, arm trap .18; elite TF 31.1%), bottom reversal / roll-through (`FS.rev`), else the referee stands them up (`FS.standUp`). Pins only from exposures (`FS.pin`, x `pinBig` for 4/5).
- Passivity: per-wrestler meter rises on quiet ticks (more for conservative / leading plans), falls when attacking; the referee acts at random on a clear gap: verbal warning first, then a 30 s activity clock (passive man x1.6 attack, opponent x.85); point to the opponent unless the passive man scores. Forced at 2:00 of the 1st if 0-0. Last 30 s: a conservative leader may be cautioned for fleeing (+1).
- Challenges: after conceding in a bout within 6, `FS.chal` 5%, overturned `FS.chalWin` 35% (takedown overturned -> back to neutral); lost = +1 to the opponent and no more challenges.
- `FS_OVR_W` / `fsOvrOfAttrs`: first-guess freestyle rating weights (not yet refitted on engine impact).
- `FS_PACE` by kg (61 1.16 ... 125 .80).

## Season (js/game/freestyle.js, 2026-11-14)
- **State**: `G.fs = { on, init, year, step, rtc: {tid: [ids]}, club, intl: {CODE: {kg: id}}, prev: {team, medal}, q, team, ev: {farrell, open, trials, worlds|olympics}, ent, moved, hist, honors, payoff, grads, lastGrads, nextId }`; post-college and foreign athletes in `G.fsw` (ids `f<n>`, graduates keep their college id), never in `G.wrestlers`. College RTC members stay in G.wrestlers (`w.fsKg` = freestyle weight). `ath(G, id)` looks in both.
- **New game**: `newGame(..., { fs: true })` only sets `G.fs = { on }`; pools are built at the first freestyle season (`initFreestyle`, own rng) -> the college world is identical with the option on (checked: JSON equal). Older careers: "Turn it on" link on the Offseason page / Freestyle page (cannot be turned off).
- **Flow**: offseason step 6 `fs` (`offSteps(G)`): `startFsSeason` (roster step) -> top-right button runs Farrell -> US Open -> Trials -> Worlds/Olympics -> start the next season. Button text matches the folkstyle "Go to tournament ▶": "Rosters set → Bill Farrell ▶", then "Go to the US Open ▶", "Go to the World/Olympic Team Trials ▶", "Go to the World Championships / Olympic Games ▶" (`evName`; 2026-11-15). Each event opens in the live tournament viewer (below) and is closed before the next one. `startNewSeason` runs anything skipped (headless sims; `finishFsSeason` closes an open event, runs + closes the rest, drops `fs.live`).
- **Pools at init**: 51 real US seniors (`FS_REAL_US`, fsdata.js: RTC school, birth year, quality tier from 2025-26 results), generated RTC athletes per school (count/level from prestige + roster strength, quality capped 16.2), 6 unattached club wrestlers per weight, one wrestler per country per weight (42 countries, `COUNTRIES` strength = average attribute of their best; top tier +0.9 after the first test). `FS_KG_OVERRIDE`: real freestyle weights of in-game college wrestlers (Bassett 65, Haines 79, Blaze 61, Kasak 70, ...). `FS_PREV`: the 2026 World Team and Astana medals (byes for the first Trials).
- **Graduates** (`graduate`, called in beginOffseason): seniors with FS OVR >= 15.6 become senior athletes at their school's RTC; AI graduates from weaker programs may move to a top-12 RTC (`(92 - prestige)/80`, .05-.6); yours stay unless you drop them.
- **AI RTCs** add their NCAA All-Americans and last year's World Team members still in college; free agents (FS OVR >= 15.5) are picked up at random by prestige 60+ RTCs. Your All-Americans and graduates start on your RTC; you add/drop freely (college wrestlers only your own), one weight move (+-1 class) per season in the roster step.
- **Entries**: you tick yours per event (default all); AI odds: Farrell .55 (.35 if already qualified, .15 with a medalist bye) + guests from 10 countries (.35 each); US Open .85 / .45 / .10. Fields measured: Farrell 14-34, US Open 13-44 per weight.
- **Brackets**: US events use `runBracket` (NCAA-style wrestlebacks); Worlds UWW single elimination (top 8 seeded) + repechage for the finalists' victims, two bronzes; Olympics: US + best 15 by rating. Per-event form noise `gauss * .9`.
- **Trials**: qualifiers = top 6 US at Farrell or US Open, last year's World Team (any weight), last season's NCAA champions, at-large by rating to 16. Last year's medalist at that weight (still at it) waits in the best-of-3 final; without one the bracket final is match 1 of the series. Winner -> World Team.
- **Season end**: honors (golds/silvers/bronzes/Olympic/US teams/US Open titles, all countries), history (US team + places, world champions), prestige payoff for the RTC's school (gold .6, silver .45, bronze .35, team .2, US Open title .1; x max(.2, 1 - prestige/120), cap 1.5), next year's `prev`. Aging: +1 attribute chance per attribute .22 (<= 24) / .10 (<= 27) / .02, 2% breakout x3; from 30, -1 chance (age-29) x .06 on speed/cardio/flex/tdo/tdd. Retirement: 37+, 33+ 45%, weak 30+/26+/24+; retired foreign spots refilled with a young wrestler (strength - .9).
- **Measured (3 seasons, seed 5, before records/carryover)**: US medals 5 / 8 (Olympics, 16-man fields) / 6 per year (real 2023-25 ~4-6, 2026: 9); Worlds fields 28-36; fs part of the save 0.35 -> 0.49 MB over 3 years (US pool grows then retirements catch up; watch it); a freestyle season + offseason ~350 ms. Freestyle off: longrun identical to before.
- **Who is "yours"** (`isMineAt(G, ev, id)`): your RTC's athletes at the Farrell, US Open and Trials; every US athlete at the Worlds / Olympics (you coach Team USA there). Their bouts are stored with seed + snapshots (`res.bouts`), listed under "Your athletes' bouts" / "Team USA bouts".
- **Live tournament viewer** (2026-11-14): `runFsEvent` still simulates the whole event up front (results, tallies, qualifications final at once; same rng as before: 6 careers x 2 seasons JSON-identical to the previous freestyle.js), and also records every bout in `fs.live = { ev, wts: FS_WEIGHTS, result: { weights: { kg: { matches } } }, snaps, rv, next, closed }`: per bout `i, l` (reveal level), `c` (championship side), `rl` (round name), `lb`, `a, b, sa, sb` (seeds), `pa, pb` (feeder bouts, -1 = none), `w, d, seed, u` (>= 0 = yours, `isMineAt`); `snaps` = one bout snapshot per athlete (replay = `simulateFsBout(snaps[a], snaps[b], {seed, kg})`). Levels: US brackets use `roundPlan` (same as folkstyle); UWW main rounds counted back from the semifinal (level 5), repechage 6-8, bronze 9, final 10; Trials final series 40+; a bout is never below its feeders + 1. Reveal = live.js (made weight-list generic: `t.wts`), same rules as folkstyle: Watch next bout / Watch next RTC (Team USA) bout / Next bout / Finish round at a weight / Finish round / Finish event; tabs Results so far (feed + placings as they are decided), Brackets (championship + consolation, or main + repechage/bronze; ▶ Watch on revealed bouts), My athletes / Team USA (record, next bout, finish, bouts). The step only moves on at `closeFsEvent` (top-right "Done -> next event"); after the Worlds / Olympics the season-end work (`endFsSeason`: honors, aging, retirements, inbox) runs then, so nothing is spoiled while watching. Records / Hall of Fame tabs are hidden while an event is being revealed. After closing, "Brackets & all bouts" on the results card reopens it until the next event runs. Checked: 11,874 bouts recorded, every feeder at a lower level, sampled replays all match; `fs.live` is up to ~145 KB of the save (one event at a time, dropped at the next college season). No start-of-period choices (freestyle has none).
- **RTC names** (2026-11-14): `js/data/rtcnames.js` `rtcName(school)` = mascot + "Wrestling Club" (Nittany Lion, Hawkeye, Cowboy Wrestling Club ...; a shared mascot gets the school in front for the smaller program: Utah Valley Wolverine, Princeton Tiger, Pitt Panther, Wyoming Cowboy ...); schools not in the table (custom leagues) stay "<School> RTC".
